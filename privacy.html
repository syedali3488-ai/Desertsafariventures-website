Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you place a booking inquiry through our website or WhatsApp, we collect minimal operational information required to coordinate your tour, including your full name, hotel/pickup address, contact phone number, and safari date.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used to dispatch 4x4 drivers, send pickup time confirmations via WhatsApp/SMS, and manage safari operations. We do not sell or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative and electronic safeguards to ensure the confidentiality of customer reservation records.
