WHAT TO EXPECT ON THE DAY

Tour Program & Itinerary

A typical evening safari schedule designed for optimal comfort and unforgettable memories.

1
03:00 PM – 03:30 PM

4x4 SUV Hotel Pickup

Your professional licensed safari driver arrives at your hotel reception in a clean, climate-controlled Toyota Land Cruiser. Enjoy a comfortable 45-minute drive to the Lahbab Red Dunes.

2
04:15 PM – 05:00 PM

Red Dune Bashing & Sandboarding

Tires deflated, seatbelts fastened! Experience thrilling dune bashing across steep red dunes. Stop at the highest dune crest for sandboarding and action photos.

3
05:15 PM – 05:45 PM

Golden Hour Sunset Photography

Capture breathtaking 360-degree desert sunset views. Perfect lighting for family portraits, couple photos, and memorable landscape shots.

4
06:00 PM – 07:30 PM

Bedouin Camp Arrival, Camel Ride & Henna

Welcome drinks, Arabic coffee & dates upon arrival. Experience camel riding, traditional henna painting, and optional quad biking or shisha lounge relaxation.

5
07:30 PM – 09:00 PM

5-Star BBQ Feast & Live Performances

Savor an elaborate BBQ buffet with grilled kebabs, chicken tikka, vegetarian specialties, and Arabic desserts, accompanied by spectacular Tanoura, Fire, and Belly Dance shows.

6
09:30 PM – 10:00 PM

Return Drop-Off to Your Hotel

Relax in the 4x4 SUV as your driver safely returns you directly to your hotel or apartment doorstep in Dubai.

We offer 100% Free Cancellation up to 24 hours prior to pickup. You do not need to pay anything advance to secure your booking. You pay on the date of your tour directly to your driver in cash (AED, USD, EUR) or by credit card.
