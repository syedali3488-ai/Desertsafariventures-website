About Desert Safari Ventures

Operated by Classic Routes Tourism L.L.C — a premier direct desert safari operator in Dubai, owning our 4x4 fleet and authentic Bedouin camps to deliver uncompromised luxury without reseller markups.

Our Story & Mission

Desert Safari Ventures (Classic Routes Tourism L.L.C) was built on a simple promise: providing authentic, high-quality Arabian desert hospitality directly to travelers without hidden agent fees or inflated prices.

From high-adrenaline dune bashing over the Lahbab Red Dunes to 5-star live BBQ dining and VIP carpeted lounge seating, we manage every step of your journey with our licensed drivers and hospitality team.
