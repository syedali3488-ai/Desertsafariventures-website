Complete Tour Itinerary & Timeline

Here is what your 6-hour Dubai desert safari experience looks like from hotel pickup to return drop-off.

1
02:30 PM – 03:00 PM

Hotel Pickup in 4x4 Land Cruiser

Your licensed driver picks you up from your hotel or residence in Dubai or Sharjah in a clean, air-conditioned 4x4 SUV.

2
04:00 PM – 04:45 PM

Lahbab Red Dunes Dune Bashing

Arrive at the high red dunes of Lahbab. Driver adjusts tire pressures and takes you on a thrilling 35-minute dune bashing experience.

3
04:45 PM – 05:30 PM

Sunset Photo Stop & Sandboarding

Stop at the highest dune peak for breathtaking golden hour sunset photography and slide down the dunes on sandboards.

4
05:30 PM – 06:30 PM

Traditional Bedouin Camp Welcome

Enter authentic desert camp with Arabic Gahwa coffee, fresh dates, short camel ride, and traditional Henna painting.

5
06:30 PM – 07:15 PM

Gourmet 5-Star International BBQ Buffet

Enjoy live BBQ grill counters featuring chicken tikka, shish tawook, kebabs, vegetarian biryani, fresh salads, hummus & desserts.

6
07:15 PM – 09:00 PM

7 Live Stage Entertainment Performances

Watch mesmerising Tanoura folk dancers, hypnotic Belly dance, thrill fire spinners, and traditional Dabke folk shows under the stars.

7
09:00 PM – 09:30 PM

Return Drop-Off to Your Hotel

Board your 4x4 SUV for a relaxed, comfortable drive back directly to your hotel doorstep.
