Operating rules and guest guidelines

1. Payment Policy

All safari bookings operate on a "Pay on Arrival" basis unless otherwise agreed. Payment can be made in cash (AED, USD, EUR, GBP) or card directly to your driver upon pickup.

2. Cancellation & Amendments

Free cancellation or date modifications are allowed up to 24 hours prior to tour departure time by notifying our support team on WhatsApp at +971 54 450 8581.

3. Safety & Health Requirements

Dune bashing is an off-road activity. Pregnant women, individuals with severe spinal ailments, or cardiac conditions should request gentle direct camp transfer.
