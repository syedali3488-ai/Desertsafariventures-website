Sunrise & Morning Desert Safari

Ideal for early risers & travelers on tight schedules. Experience pristine desert dunes as the sun rises over the red sands of Lahbab. Includes 4x4 dune bashing, camel trek, sandboarding, & refreshments.

Morning Safari Schedule (4 Hours)

  • 5:00 AM - 5:30 AM: Hotel pickup in private or sharing 4x4 SUV
  • 6:15 AM: Sunrise photo stop on untouched crest of red dunes
  • 6:45 AM: 40 minutes of high-thrill Lahbab dune bashing
  • 7:30 AM: Sandboarding & morning camel ride session
  • 8:15 AM: Arabic coffee, dates & cold beverages
  • 9:30 AM: Return drop-off at your hotel in time for breakfast
