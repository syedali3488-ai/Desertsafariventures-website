Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your Dubai desert safari tour.

What should I wear for the desert safari? −
We recommend comfortable casual clothing such as cotton shirts, shorts, or trousers. Sandals or sneakers are best for sandboarding. During winter months (Nov–March), bring a light jacket for the evening.
Is hotel pickup included in the price? +
Yes! Free 4x4 Land Cruiser hotel pickup and drop-off is included in Evening Premium (AED 119) and VIP Luxury (AED 249) packages anywhere in Dubai or Sharjah.
Are vegetarian, vegan, and Halal food options available? +
Absolutely. All food served is 100% certified Halal. Our 5-star buffet includes separate live vegetarian counters featuring grilled paneer, dal, vegetable biryani, hummus, fresh salads, and falafel.
Is dune bashing safe for pregnant women or infants? +
Dune bashing is not recommended for pregnant women, infants under 3, or guests with severe back/neck conditions. We offer direct gentle camp transfer without dune bashing upon request.
What is your cancellation policy? +
You can cancel or reschedule free of charge up to 24 hours before your safari time. Since you pay cash on arrival to your driver, there are zero upfront cancellation fees.
