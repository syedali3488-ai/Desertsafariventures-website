Evening Premium Red Dune Safari

Our flagship 6-hour desert adventure in Lehbab High Red Dunes. Includes 4x4 pickup from your Dubai hotel, exhilarating dune bashing, sandboarding, 5-star 3-course BBQ dinner, and 7 live Arabian cultural shows.

Tour Overview & Highlights

The Evening Premium Safari is crafted for guests who want the complete, uncompromised Dubai desert experience. You will be picked up directly from your hotel or residence in a clean, air-conditioned 4x4 Land Cruiser. Head into the deep Lahbab Red Dunes desert where your licensed desert driver will navigate steep dunes for 30–35 minutes of heart-pumping dune bashing.
